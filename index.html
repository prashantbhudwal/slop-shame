For the LinkedIn home feed

A little less slop.
A little more fun.

Slop Shame gives feed posts a 0–100 Slop Index based on familiar AI-style writing patterns.

In development. Chrome Web Store availability will be linked here after release.

Read the signals. Decide for yourself.

Local scoring highlights writing patterns and explains the score. Destroy adds a burst of drama as you hide an ordinary post from your view. Eligible posts can become satirical cards, generated from bundled templates.

A score is a heuristic, not proof of AI authorship. Human writing can score highly, and AI writing can score low.

Your feed stays on your device.

Post text and scores are processed locally, without an AI service or developer backend. Local author rankings stay in your browser. Author photos can load from LinkedIn when cards are viewed or exported; see the privacy policy for details.

Share thoughtfully.

Card exports start with initials and pixelated photos. Quotes and other context can still identify a person. Nothing is posted automatically.
