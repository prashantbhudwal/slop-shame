Scales by
Sticker size 24→42 px, tilt 0°→−14°, and the tier art itself (the mug vs the mushroom cloud).
How we’d build it
An <img> of the existing tier art in the decoration host, at the card’s measured top-left, with a white die-cut edge from stacked drop-shadows. It overhangs the corner into the feed gutter; if a card’s frame clips overflow, it tucks fully inside at 4,4 instead. It covers the photo’s corner, never text.

My pick

Ship 5 · Drip Edge as the main signal. The top edge is the first part of a post to scroll into view, so it is the only option you can read before your eye reaches the author. It stays in the card’s top padding (text is never covered), it is one edge and not a frame, and it scales on three non-colour axes (reach, drip count, drip length), so Faint Whiff is one quiet bead and SLOPOCALYPSE is unmistakable in both themes. It is also pure brand: the same slime as Sloppy and the tier art.

Offer 1 · Slop Gauge as the calm alternative in settings for anyone who finds the goo too much: the same “how much” reading as a 3–6 px strip in the left padding, the least intrusive thing on this page, and it survives the squint test almost as well. I would retire the avatar ring and monster rather than stack them on top; the chip keeps the score, the panel, Destroy and Shame.
