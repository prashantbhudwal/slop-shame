# Calibration data manifest

Verified 2026-10-19 from the primary sources below. This is a **general English
AI-text detection** corpus. Genres and registers drive evaluation; the extension's
initial host platform does not define the task. No corpus is shipped in the
extension. Dataset text retains its own license; the MIT code license does not
relicense third-party samples.

`sources.lock.json` records exact input URLs, source-byte SHA-256 values, normalized
file hashes, and counts. Large downloads and all restricted or uncleared text
stay in ignored `data/eval/raw/`. A `LicenseRef-…` below means custom terms or
unresolved source rights, **not** an invented SPDX license granting permission.

No separate host-platform corpus was sought. No artificially written text is
labeled human. Dataset human labels and pre-November-2022 dates are useful
provenance, not a literal authorship guarantee: automated accounts, older text
generators, quotations, and dataset errors predate ChatGPT. No native-language
status is inferred from a person's name or academic affiliation.

## Normalization and grouping

Every JSONL row has `{id,text,label,source,subset,words,genre,register}` and optional
provenance fields. Length is 30–600 ECMAScript whitespace-delimited tokens, not a
linguistic tokenizer or the engine's possibly different word count. Do not correct
human spelling/grammar or remove tells before scoring. Other than official-release
paragraph excerpts, oversize texts are excluded rather than cut or summarized.

`subset` tracks hard-negative membership or positive provenance. `genre` tracks
text form; `register` tracks register, not platform. Genre annotations on W&I are
coarse salutation-based labels; learner register is deliberately separate from
native formal text. Hard-human FP pools **only human** `formal` + `non-native`
subsets, and reports them separately too. A mixed/AI formal-register item never
enters that denominator. Empty cells stay `n/a`.

The corpus loader removes exact NFKC/whitespace-normalized duplicate texts across
sources and refuses conflicting labels or duplicate IDs. Similar FOMC templates,
RAID parallel generations, and synthetic pairs are still correlated. Future
calibration/holdout splits must group by original document/prompt/pair (and by
learner author when available), not shuffle rows independently. This corpus is a
calibration set, not an untouched validation set, and has English/genre/model and
length-selection bias. Report per-source, per-genre and per-register counts with
every result; two product-review negatives cannot establish safety in that genre.
