# Reviewer instructions — draft

Revalidate these steps on the final uploaded version. No paid service or Slop Shame account is needed. Live feed testing requires a reviewer-controlled LinkedIn account; do not supply the owner's credentials. If Google requires test access, arrange it through the dashboard's secure process and LinkedIn's permitted account practices.

1. Install the uploaded extension, open https://www.linkedin.com/feed/ and sign in to LinkedIn normally.
2. Refresh the feed after installing. Scroll normally. Visible feed cards receive Slop Index badges; expand a badge to inspect the matched signals. The index is a heuristic, not a claim of certain AI authorship.
3. On a non-promoted post, select Destroy. The effect hides it in this browser view; it does not delete the original LinkedIn post. Promoted posts do not offer Destroy.
4. On a qualifying post, select Shame. Eligibility is determined locally; not every post offers this action. If no eligible post is visible, continue normal browsing. Do not automate LinkedIn interactions to manufacture one.
5. Inspect a card, copy text, or download its PNG. Identity sharing starts off per card: initials in exported text, initials and pixelation in the PNG. Opt-in changes exports to full identity. Quoted content can still be identifying. No card is sent automatically.
6. Open the toolbar popup for settings, today's counters, and local top authors. Disable the extension there and confirm feed controls stop.
7. Review permission scope: storage plus https://www.linkedin.com/*. Local scoring does not call an AI service or developer API. Opening/exporting a card may load the existing author image from LinkedIn's image host.

The local database retains author aggregates and short post snippets. Uninstall removes browser-managed extension data. There is currently no shipped Hall of Slop full-page/clear control; do not claim or test one until implemented.

Before submission: record exact extension version, supported browser version, and the results of these steps. Add real screenshots from that same version. Fixture tests are available in the development repository but do not replace a working uploaded package or reviewer-access instructions.
